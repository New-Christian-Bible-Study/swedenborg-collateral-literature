== Section Level two
[.text-center]

_Italicize with underbars_ ---
*Bold with asterisks* ---
`Monospace with back-ticks` ---
#Highlight with a pair of number/pound signs# ---
~Subscript~ with tilde, and ^Superscript^ with carats ---
*_Roles_*, such as [.underline]#underline#, are delimited by the number (#) sign, and declared by naming the role, preceded by dot, within square brackets, preceeding the initial delimiter.

=== level three
////

The pound {#} sign is of special use demarcating strings +
The back-tick {`} grave is of special use demarcating "code blocks" +
The plus {+} sign is used for complex "passthrough" blocks. +
The caret {^} superscripts, and the tilde {~} subscripts

--
Two dashes; "Open block" +
~sub~Normal^super^, `Mono`, _Italic_, *Bold*, [.underline]#Underline#, #Highlight#
--

----
Four dashes, or dots; "Listing block" +
~sub~Normal^super^, `Mono`, _Italic_, *Bold*, [.underline]#Underline#, #Highlight#
----

****
asterisks; four of them +
~sub~Normal^super^, `Mono`, _Italic_, *Bold*, [.underline]#Underline#, #Highlight#
****

Normal Text +
~sub~Normal^super^, `Mono`, _Italic_, *Bold*, [.underline]#Underline#, #Highlight#

====
Four equals signs; "Example block" +
~sub~Normal^super^, `Mono`, _Italic_, *Bold*, [.underline]#Underline#, #Highlight#
====
